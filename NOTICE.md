# Attribution and reuse

UnionMimic is an independent engineering and research adaptation, not an official release or endorsement of Sharpa Robotics or the SuperMimic authors.

WM-Craftnet supplies the task, sensors, model and released checkpoint. SuperMimic supplies the methodological reference. The LEAP adaptation supplies a learner-code reference. [Pinned versions and checksums](config/upstreams.json) record the exact sources.

Upstream source, simulator packages and weights are downloaded separately. Their licenses and access terms continue to apply. Review them before redistributing derived code, weights or assets; this repository does not grant additional rights to upstream materials.

Original graphics in `docs/assets/` and `website/assets/` were created for UnionMimic. Website art is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph or experimental result. The design takes cues from the warm, spacious editorial presentation of [Physical Intelligence](https://www.pi.website/) and [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/); it does not reuse their logos, images or copy. There is no affiliation with either company.

A project-wide reuse license has not yet been selected. Repository visibility does not itself grant an open-source license.

## Published experimental artifacts (9 November 2026)

The committed SM demonstration checkpoint was trained by this project using the pinned LEAP-derived learner and Sharpa simulation task. Geometry readouts and random encoder controls were fitted/generated by this project; the original released WM-Craftnet weights remain a separate pinned download. Sharpa reference previews and retarget trajectories derive from the cited Wuji pen references; they do not certify physical tracking.

Copies of applicable upstream notices are preserved in `docs/upstream-notices/`. These notices do not relicense third-party materials. The project-specific changes include Sharpa controller/state adapters, geometry readouts, error calibration, and labeled result/demo rendering. See `docs/RESULTS_RELEASE_20261109.md` for the exact publication manifest.
